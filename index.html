Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Further Engine.

What is Further Engine?
Further Engine is an enhanced, next-generation fork of Psych Engine — the popular Friday Night Funkin' mod engine. It adds powerful online features like global leaderboards, account systems, level progression, and modern optimizations on top of the core engine.
What does Further Engine add over regular Psych Engine?
Further Engine includes: online accounts & logins (web + in-game), global real-time leaderboards, XP and level system, automatic score syncing, cross-platform profiles, optimized Android builds, and improved performance across the board.
How do I create an account?
You can register directly on this website by clicking the Register button. You can also create an account directly in-game. Once registered, the same credentials work everywhere.
Is an account required to play?
No. You can play completely offline without an account. However, you won't be able to access leaderboards or cloud features until you register. Accounts can be created anytime.
How does the level system work?
Level is calculated based on your total score across all songs. Level = √(Total Score / 1000). Only your highest score on each song/difficulty counts towards your total progress.
Can I use mods with Further Engine?
Yes! Further Engine is 100% compatible with Lua and HScript mods. Just place them in your /mods folder. Note: Online scores are only tracked for official base game songs.
Is Further Engine free?
Completely free and open source. No ads, no paywalls. Everything is available to everyone. The source code is fully public on GitHub.
What is Further Engine?
Further Engine has more customization and features than the normal Psych Engine version. It aims to provide a better experience for players. It is currently in Beta — there may be some bugs in the game.
Is multiplayer / online rooms available?
No. Online room creation and multiplayer features are NOT available yet. They will be added soon! The game has a login system and scores turn into UP points that go to the global leaderboard.
How does the leaderboard work?
Points earned in-game are converted to Ultra Points (UP) and sent to the global leaderboard. Players compete for the top ranks!
